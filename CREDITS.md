# Credits

Everyone who touched this, in plain words.

- **The VICE Team**: the Versatile Commodore Emulator, the engine under Nix64.
  https://vice-emu.sourceforge.io (GPL-2.0-or-later)
- **Dag Lem** and the reSID authors: SID chip emulation shipped inside VICE.
- **Fred Nix (nixfred)**: product direction, brand and every design decision.
- **Larry:Prime** (Claude, Anthropic): foundation code, research and tests.
- **Ken Bandoly** (@Maverick_229, github MaverickKB): the first outside verification, Chuck Yeager's
  Advanced Flight Trainer running on Nix64, and a community recipe for it.
  https://github.com/MaverickKB/omarchy-chuck-yeager-nix64 (MIT)
- **@MaddenFilmRoom0**: the download page of C64 collections (https://tech.mfr.st/downloads) the ROM library was built and tested against.
  Nix64 hosts and ships none of it.

Hardware and software projects Nix64 builds on or talks to will be added here as they are
integrated: the High Voltage SID Collection and the PSID file format (the SID jukebox reads their files), OpenCBM, ZoomFloppy/XUM1541, USBSID-Pico, WiC64, libsidplayfp, CCGMS, and the
BBS operators who keep their boards online.
